Official machine-readable OpenAPI specification for the **AQX Logistics Public Rate API**, powering cross-border air freight calculation, dimensional repacking benchmarks, and landed cost estimation.

## ✈️ Target Markets Covered (15 Corridors)
1. **GCC**: Saudi Arabia (`SA`), United Arab Emirates (`AE`), Kuwait (`KW`), Qatar (`QA`), Bahrain (`BH`), Oman (`OM`).
2. **Europe**: United Kingdom (`GB`), Germany (`DE`), France (`FR`), Spain (`ES`), Netherlands (`NL`), Sweden (`SE`), Portugal (`PT`).
3. **Oceania**: Australia (`AU`), New Zealand (`NZ`).

## 💎 Pricing Logic
* **Chargeable Weight $\ge 10.0$ kg (or 10x10x10" box)**: **$8.50 / kg + 15% VAT DDP** across ALL categories.
* **Chargeable Weight $< 10.0$ kg**: **$15.50 / kg + 15% VAT DDP** (0.5 kg minimum).
